How it works

Practice for the moments that matter.

I CAN makes difficult conversations approachable. Students work through realistic scenarios, discuss their options, and leave with responses they can use in everyday life.

01

Recognize the pressure

Students learn to identify bullying and peer pressure before a situation escalates.

02

Practice a response

Small-group activities turn abstract advice into words and actions students can remember.

03

Build lasting confidence

Students strengthen self-advocacy, perseverance, and the courage to ask for help.

Our journey

One idea. A growing impact.

What began as a local library seminar has grown into a student-led program reaching hundreds of young people.

2024

The first workshop

Prisha founded I CAN and hosted the first library seminar with her teacher, Sensei Sarah.

2025

The team grows

Shrihan joined Prisha for an August library seminar, and their work was featured in the Community Advocate.

2026

Reaching 500+ students

Prisha and Shrihan delivered their first large-scale school workshop for more than 500 students.
